HOW TO READ THE REVISED FLOWCHART
Read stages 1 and 2 first. Assessment results feed two separate paths:
flagged/unresolved items enter stage 3; all required assessments and current
movement versions feed the monthly close in stage 4. Human review is not a
mandatory manual step for every movement. Corrections trigger reassessment.
Dashed arrows show feedback and the offline model lifecycle. All PostgreSQL
shapes denote logical tables in the same operational database, not separate
database servers. Blue denotes processing, green durable data, purple model
operations and amber human actions. The darker green cylinder stores results.

Logistic Regression runs after point-in-time feature construction, using a
frozen model and a versioned threshold policy. Store every assessment, not
only flags: subject/version, score, status, rule/model/policy versions, feature
reference, cutoff and evaluation time. Missing scores are NULL, never zero.
Deterministic failures persist and trigger alerts without waiting for a
successful model evaluation. A low model score cannot clear a failed control.
Human outcomes remain separate evidence and supply reviewed training labels.

HOW TO READ THE OVERVIEW
Blue: processing/services. Teal: durable data and evidence. Purple: analytics
and offline training. Amber: authenticated human actions. Solid connectors
show operational flow; dashed amber connectors show human commands.
Boxes describe logical responsibilities, not a mandatory number of servers.
The overview compresses detailed sequencing; the Mermaid sequences define
the transaction, issuance and external-call boundaries precisely.

CONFIRMED CONTEXT AND OPEN ASSUMPTIONS
- UK company; approximately 200 customers.
- Only the largest customer is reported to have up to 2 million movements.
- The period is interpreted as monthly; total volume and peak traffic are unknown.
- The meaning, schema, authority and delivery cadence of the two sources are unknown.
- Continuous controls depend on source availability; monthly-only inputs cannot
  support earlier detection.
- Monthly invoicing, human approval and email alerts for new flags are requirements.
- One monthly customer delivery does not necessarily mean one legal invoice.
- Invoice/VAT invoice/receipt/statement, principal-versus-agent treatment,
  billable amounts, tax rules, numbering and the issuance event require agreement.

NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN INVARIANTS
1. Preserve raw observations. Human corrections append versioned business state.
2. Source-record idempotency is separate from matching business operations.
3. Decimal accounting and explicit currency/rounding rules govern amounts.
4. A model score cannot override a failed deterministic accounting control.
5. A missing score, missing input or unknown send outcome is never treated as zero.
6. Freeze exact record/rule versions and reconcile coverage before monthly close.
7. Final document number, date, content and PDF are fixed before approval.
8. Legal issuance is an explicit domain transition, independent of transport.
   Enforce stable billable-component allocation uniqueness; version changes do
   not authorize rebilling. Adjustments use distinct authorized components.
9. Approval binds the exact PDF, sender, recipient roles, message and attachments.
10. Revocation and dispatch reservation serialize through the same lock boundary.
11. SQL and object storage do not share one atomic transaction. Verify uploaded
    objects before publishing references; reconcile orphan objects separately.
12. An outbox prevents lost logical jobs, not physically exactly-once email.
    Ambiguous external submissions and expired in-flight leases need reconciliation.
13. Provider acceptance, delivery to a mail server, opening signals and explicit
    customer acknowledgement remain independent evidence dimensions.
14. The baseline sends one logical alert per newly raised flag. Replays do not
    create new flags. Grouping or digest policies require an explicit decision.

VALIDATION SCOPE
The one-page overview is generated as vector SVG and visually inspected
after rasterization. Cardinalities, version references, source-of-truth boundaries
and email/issuance semantics received a separate conceptual review.
All five Mermaid files pass parser validation with Mermaid 11.12.0. This checks
syntax, not the visual layout produced by every Mermaid editor. The validation
receipt includes the exact source SHA-256 hashes.
The revised flowchart 01 was also rendered locally with Mermaid 11.12.0 and
headless Chromium and visually inspected for readable labels and routing.
No customer database was inspected, no invoice was issued and no email was sent.
This pack does not establish throughput, model quality or tax compliance.
